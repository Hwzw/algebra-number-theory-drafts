# Five selected research manuscripts

Revised 8 September 2026. These are five consolidated algebra and number-theory manuscripts, selected for general structural results and reusable consequences. Four substantially strengthen the earlier drafts; P06 retains its conjecture resolutions and asymptotic, with corrected source pinpoints. The original thirteen drafts remain in [original collection](../README.md#original-thirteen-drafts).

**Status:** full mathematical manuscripts with internal AI proof and source audits. No human expert review, journal acceptance, guaranteed priority, or predicted citation impact is claimed. Each assessment states the nearest prior results, source-access limits, and the case for specialist significance. Compilation and conversion checks are separate from mathematical review.

“Independent” here means a separate AI agent reconstructed the argument; it does not mean an external human referee. Internal reviewers found no remaining mathematical gap in these final statements. Exact finite checks support the hand proofs and are not premises of their infinite conclusions. A bounded literature search cannot exclude every prior or unpublished result.

The selection does not count each corollary as another paper. P01 combines the conjecture and all operator consequences. P02 combines the matrix theorem, recurrence theorem, Lucas factor and universal exponent. P06 is distinct because its central results concern cancellations and asymptotics of one scalar combinatorial sequence, which the uniform matrix classification does not determine. P09 combines the finite-length theorem and its examples. P13 combines the radical theorem with its applications.
