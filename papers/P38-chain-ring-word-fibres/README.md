# Word fibres over finite chain rings and the Amit--Ashurst conjecture for metacyclic groups

**Henry Zweiman** · September 9, 2026

This eight-page proposed research paper proves the Amit--Ashurst lower bound for all words on all finite metacyclic p-groups, including nonsplit extensions, and on all elementary-abelian-by-cyclic p-groups. A general chain-ring estimate and a central cyclic cover give both results. The general conjecture for finite nilpotent groups remains unresolved.
