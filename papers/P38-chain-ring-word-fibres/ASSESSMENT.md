# Problem, prior work, and significance assessment

Henry Zweiman. September 9, 2026. Internal assessment of one proposed research manuscript, not an external priority certificate.

## Published problem and solved scope

The Amit--Ashurst conjecture asks whether every nonempty fibre of every k-variable word on a finite nilpotent group G has at least |G|^(k-1) tuples. It is Ashurst's 2012 thesis Conjecture 6.2.1, restated in Camina--Cocke--Thillaisundaram (2023), Section 1, and Chlebus--Cocke--Ho (2024), Question 4.2. The manuscript proves this assertion for every metacyclic p-group, hence every nilpotent metacyclic group, and for every elementary-abelian-by-cyclic p-group. These are broad cases of an established open conjecture, not a new conjecture invented solely for this manuscript.

The common mechanism is a theorem for scalar principal-unit actions over finite commutative chain rings. A separate transfer within the same proof removes splitting when the quotient is cyclic. The general nilpotent-group conjecture is not solved. No statement about nonnilpotent metacyclic groups is made.

The 2023 final article's title alone could suggest a complete metacyclic result; its actual main theorem has the cyclic-maximal hypothesis. The example C16 semidirect C4 with action 3 has class four, exponent sixteen and no cyclic subgroup of order 32. It is therefore outside that main theorem and outside class-two results. The manuscript proves these structural facts directly.

## Priority search and its limits

The focused search included the exact conjecture name with metacyclic, abelian-by-cyclic, elementary abelian, chain ring, and 2024--2026; author publication pages; the 2023 paper's indexed citing works; and the July 2026 wreath-product paper. One citation-index query found only Kishnani--Kulshrestha; the index is incomplete, as the separately located Chlebus--Cocke--Ho article illustrates. No source inspected in this audit states the two main theorems in their full scope.

The 2024 Kent State seminar title about latest metacyclic results was located, but its listing gives no theorem statement. It does not establish either a priority conflict or novelty. The final corrected Kishnani--Kulshrestha body was not retrieved in the earlier investigation; available scope is class two. These limits are recorded rather than silently treated as exhaustive clearance. No author contact or private communication was attempted.

## Why this is a substantial single contribution

The all-metacyclic result removes a real subgroup-index restriction from a published theorem. The elementary-abelian-by-cyclic result covers arbitrary module rank and groups of unbounded class, including every cyclic regular wreath product. The chain-ring estimate retains the image size of the exponent-sum map, enabling the nonsplit transfer. Its exact description of the word image over each quotient value may support later work on word distributions.

These related statements constitute one paper. A citation count cannot be predicted or guaranteed. Significance is assessed from the established conjecture, removal of hypotheses, and reusable method, not from the number of computations or pages.
