## Why this is more than a leading eigenvalue estimate

The leading row estimate and approximate-identity argument are elementary in isolation. The contribution is the complete combination: a noncompact strong limit, collapse of every fixed eigenvalue to the maximum width, escape of every fixed weight, and convergence of the full input spectral measure. A separate energy argument controls the perfect-reaction endpoint, allowing relative error uniformly across an unbounded reactivity range. This then gives an exact criterion for asymptotic validity of the standard one-pole approximation and a nonzero limiting error for every nonconstant width profile. The tube remainders and connected weight-reversal example settle additional explicit questions without being split into separate papers.

These results give reusable local spectral and response data for the active narrow-capture literature. They also expose the distinction between eigenvalue location and observable spectral mass, which is relevant when replacing a full response by a principal mode. These are concrete reasons to seek expert analysis/PDE review at the program's desired standard. The proof uses established tools and is relatively short; a journal may regard its breadth or technical depth as better suited to a specialized analysis or applied-mathematics venue. Citation impact and journal suitability cannot be certified.
