Every interior binomial coefficient is at least the degree, giving a uniform rounding error at most one over twice the degree. 3. **Weighted lifting.** The cutoff is identically zero in neighborhoods of every zero of the denominator, including both endpoints. The quotient therefore extends continuously, with the chosen integer endpoint values. Transitions occur only where the original function lies strictly inside the required bounds. Positive linear endpoint margins and compactness give a global margin proportional to t(1−t). The approximation perturbation contains the same factor, so a sufficiently small uniform error preserves strict inequalities throughout the open interval. Real repeated roots cause no additional issue because whole neighborhoods are removed from the quotient.

4. **Endpoint arithmetic.** Integer linear corrections realize the two endpoint residues simultaneously. The identity embedding is included when the parameter itself is in the open unit interval. Values zero and one are separately admitted. Increasing a box in those dimensions makes its volume arbitrarily large while physical coordinates remain arbitrarily small and internal coordinates remain bounded by a fixed admissible margin. 7. **Projection density and the model set.** The trace pairing identifies the full dual lattice with a lattice of field elements, including complex coordinates with the factor two and no accidental conjugation.
