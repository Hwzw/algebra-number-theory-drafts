## Proposed advance

The manuscript answers Remark 3.7 for arbitrary fixed positive r by keeping strict inequalities homogeneous and including every mixed-sum closure constraint. It then determines the universal leading coefficient and the parity oscillation of the next coefficient. These yield eventual strict monotonicity for each fixed r, a partial result toward Conjecture 1.5.

Cyrusian and Kaplan already use rational polyhedra and, for r=2, a bulk with exceptional additive equalities. Those ideas are credited antecedents. The proposed additional argument works for arbitrary r and observes that the relevant exceptional hyperplanes pass through the origin, forcing their top-dimensional correction coefficient to be constant. This controls consecutive differences; an unstructured error estimate would not suffice.

## Significance and scope

The strongest reason for a distinct paper is the combination of an explicit published question with an all-r partial result on semigroup enumeration monotonicity. It provides a reusable separation between a full-dimensional counting region and exceptional additive loci. Potential further work includes full periods, the constant part of the next coefficient, effective thresholds, and all-genus injections.

This remains a focused contribution, not a resolution of total semigroup-count monotonicity or the full fixed-ordinarization conjecture. No citation-count forecast, independent correctness certification, journal acceptance, or confirmed historical priority is asserted. One consolidated manuscript is counted; its individual theorems are not separate papers.
