# Open status, literature, and significance

Date: September 9, 2026. Author: Henry Zweiman. Status: internally checked proposed theorem for two infinite families; historical priority and outside mathematical assessment remain provisional.

## The precise published target

Bashir, Geroldinger, and Zhong, [On a zero-sum problem arising from factorization theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.10094), Conjecture 1.1, asks for consecutive factorization lengths over the signed support of every longest atom in a finite abelian group other than the cyclic and elementary two-group cases. The chapter appeared in 2021, [DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-67996-5_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-67996-5_2).

The equivalent invariant formulation is still explicitly Conjecture 2.3 in Doniyor Yazdonov, [On the structure of length sets with maximal elasticity, version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21383v2), revised February 25, 2026. The source's density results and its Property P do not prove the assertion for every individual longest-atom support. Its standing-conjecture statement and the relevant theorem hypotheses were checked directly.

P29 proves the signed-support assertion for noncyclic groups C2^a + C4^b + C8^c whenever a+c is not 1 modulo 3, or c=1. It also proves that only distances 1 and 3 can occur for arbitrary groups of this form. The remaining exponent-eight cases are not declared solved.

## Closest established results and dependencies

- The 2021 theorem already covers rank-two and homocyclic cases, a coprime-parameter condition, and two-level p-groups under a divisibility condition on the exponent indices. In particular the order-four/order-eight indices are 2 and 3, so that condition does not cover C4^b + C8 for arbitrary b. Both the preprint theorem and the author's 2021 theorem poster retain that condition.
- [Geroldinger and Zhong, Long sets of lengths with maximal elasticity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06907), Canadian Journal of Mathematics 70 (2018), Lemma 3.10, is used as an external structural lemma. Its full proof and exact hypotheses were read. P29 uses its non-half-order relation criterion only with least multiplier 2 and element order 8, and also uses its basis-support conclusion.
- The signed-atom length divisibility, Davenport formula for p-groups, and truncated group algebra are established tools. P29 gives short self-contained arguments for its precise uses of these tools.
- [Geroldinger and Yang, On a classical zero-sum invariant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19090v1), August 19, 2026, was checked through its relevant Section 3 results and proofs. Its group-algebra identities and order-of-terms consequences are credited; no novelty is claimed for the group-algebra method. The result sought here concerns a different signed-support distance invariant.

Targeted searches for the exponent-eight maximal-elasticity case and the C4/C8 support case did not locate a prior theorem settling these full families. This is a primary-source-informed search, not an exhaustive proof of priority. The explicit general conjecture does not itself establish that each subclass was previously open.

## Contribution and potential use

The main structural contribution is the classification of order-eight support terms when every atom length is two modulo three, together with the elimination of lower-order terms by extremal augmentation degree. It treats arbitrary numbers of order-two and order-four factors when the order-eight rank is one. The broader filtration bound identifies distance three as the sole residual obstruction for all exponent-eight groups.

The consequence for transfer Krull monoids is a uniform interval description of maximal-elasticity length sets. Thus the result connects a zero-sum support theorem to factorization invariants of monoids and domains. Extending the support classification to several order-eight coordinates is a concrete route for further work. No citation-count forecast is made.
