# Source, novelty, and significance assessment

Audit date: September 9, 2026. Author: Henry Zweiman.

## Precisely proposed result

The main theorem proves standard-weight agreement for every monomial
Simis ideal with one irreducible primary component per associated prime,
all of height three. Its converse is the established invariance of the
Simis property under standard weighting. This is the unmixed height-three
case of the MPV conjecture. The manuscript does not assert the conjecture
in arbitrary height or with mixed component heights.

## Primary statements checked

   monomial ideals*, [arXiv:2402.08833v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08833v3).
   Conjecture 5.7 states the general weighting problem. Introduction
   definitions specify distinct radicals in a minimal irreducible
   decomposition. Corollary 5.5(b) proves weighting invariance. These
   portions, including the corollary proof, were read. The journal
   metadata is J. Algebra Appl. 24 (2025), 2541001; theorem numbering here
   refers to the archived arXiv version, not an unread final journal body.
2. **Bordoloi, Das, Kumar**, *The Mendez–Pinto–Villarreal conjecture for
   some classes of monomial ideals*,
   [arXiv:2607.25683v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25683v1), July 2026.
   Conjecture 1.1 still poses the general problem. Theorem 3.10 resolves
   support-three ideals in that paper's setup; its forest results give
   another family. Its hypotheses concern generator supports, including
   the stated support-family condition. Our height-three condition is on
   the primary components. We do not claim these previously solved
   generator-support classes as new.
3. **Bijender, Ajay Kumar**, *Waldschmidt constant of monomial ideals and
   Simis ideals*, [arXiv:2512.22940v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22940v1).
   Theorems 4.7 and 4.9 treat a component-separation hypothesis and the

   dependencies were checked. The height-two case is already in

   [publisher PDF](https://imar.ro/journals/Mathematical_Reports/Pdfs/2014/1/6.pdf).
4. **Branco Correia–Zarzuela**, *Some asymptotic properties of the Rees
   powers of a module*,
   [arXiv:math/0408351v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408351v1),
   Theorem 6.4, supplies the classical complete-intersection criterion.
   The rank-one ideal-module hypothesis holds because the ideal has
   grade at least two in a regular local ring. Section 6 and the

   [arXiv:0708.2225v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2225v1), Theorem 5.3,

5. **Grisalde–Seceleanu–Villarreal**, *Research in the Mathematical

   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40687-021-00310-2), Section 7, develops

## What appears new

The proposed contribution is a uniform primary-exponent rigidity theorem
in unmixed height three. Its key steps are the reduction of any discrepancy
to five variables, use of a classical dimension-one theorem to force
four-variable rigidity, the resulting short support classification, and
an explicit all-parameter obstructing vertex for its final configuration.
The proof does not assume equal degrees or fixed generator supports.

Targeted searches used the conjecture name together with “height three,”
“height 3,” “unmixed,” and “codimension 3,” and searches for normally
torsion-free generically complete intersection monomial ideals. No
existing theorem resolving this whole height-three class was located.
The July 2026 primary source confirms the continuing general problem.
The exact height-three subproblem is an identifiable case of that named
conjecture, rather than a separately numbered question in the source.
Searches and source comparisons do not certify exhaustive priority.

## Significance and limits

This is a structural partial solution of an active named conjecture.
It advances the primary-height frontier beyond the classical height-two
case and gives a local reduction that could support higher-height work.
That offers a plausible basis for later use, but citation counts and
journal acceptance cannot be predicted. The argument leaves squarefree
Simis classification, mixed heights, higher heights, and a possible
second-power criterion unresolved.
