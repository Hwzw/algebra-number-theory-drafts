# Expert review brief

Prepared 17 September 2026. This brief has not been sent to anyone.

The attached manuscript attempts a complete planar proof for $u''+u'/r-u+|u|^{p-1}u=0$, all $p>1$, with one positive-central decaying solution for every prescribed node count. It reconstructs CGY11's comparison proof and explicitly supplies two planar/infinite-endpoint arguments. It is not represented as a novel result.

## Most important questions

**Update:** Zhang–Zhang, [arXiv:2606.17840v2, Section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17840v2#S6), explicitly claims this same planar extension. Its Section 6 is unchanged from v1, although the abstract's planar sentence was removed. Please include this exact argument in the literature/gap assessment. See the [assessment](ASSESSMENT.md) for the version comparison.

1. **Literature status.** CGY11 Theorem 1.1 and its example explicitly include the full planar power case. Is a mathematical gap or correction known? If so, which exact lemma or inference fails? Tang's later conjecture concerns both whole-space and finite-ball statements; could the discrepancy be a difference in scope rather than a failure of the planar whole-space result?
2. **Transfer lemma.** Does manuscript Lemma 5.1 correctly propagate the common-level strict comparison through every extremum? Please check the first-intersection cases, endpoint passage, and the uniform neighborhood for *every pair* of nearby parameters.
3. **Tail.** Does the preserved gap $W_2-W_1\geq\delta>0$ in Proposition 6.1 correctly rule out all alternatives except a new node on the upper side and negative-energy trapping on the lower side?

## Exact locations

- CGY11, published p. 614, Proposition 4.3: $H'=-4(n-2)F/f$ vanishes at $n=2$, so the printed strict inequality between $H(\alpha_1)$ and $H(\alpha_2)$ is unavailable.
- Manuscript equation (5.4): compare at $s=\alpha_1$ instead, obtaining $Q_1(\alpha_1)=0>Q_2(\alpha_1)$.
- CGY11, published p. 614, Proposition 4.4: the endpoint is infinite for a bound state.
- Manuscript equations (6.2)-(6.3): retain a fixed positive $W$ gap while both decaying endpoints would have $W\to0$.

The manuscript includes the variation intersection lemma, elementary ODE preliminaries, interval topology, and verification of the prescribed-node existence theorem's hypotheses. The exact identities were symbolically checked; the logical comparison still needs independent scrutiny.

## Publication decision

A correct reconstruction is not automatically a new solution. If the older theorem is accepted, this could at most be an explanatory or corrective note, subject to editorial interest. If a substantive known gap remains, its precise identification is needed before evaluating whether this argument closes it. No priority or new-paper-count claim should be made merely because the argument has been typeset.
