## Mathematical read scope

Section 6 was read in full and compared against Tang's phase-transition proof, especially Lemma 5.6 and the order of Steps 1–5 in Section 5.7. Its central observation is that Tang's auxiliary $Q_n$ equals $Q_2$ in dimension two; the latter is positive under the hypotheses established before the final $T_2$ step. At a possible negative-side minimum of $T_2$, a paired-level integral has nonnegative integrand and a positive integral, providing the needed positivity. The strict decrease of $P/r^2=2E$ supplies the other planar replacement.

This is a statement of the read scope and proposed mechanism, not an independent verification of every dependency in the full Zhang–Zhang/Tang argument. No claim of peer review or journal acceptance is inferred from an arXiv posting.
