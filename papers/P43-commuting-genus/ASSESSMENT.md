## Internal decision

The completed argument supports publication as one substantial research preprint. The originating assistant's proof audit found no remaining gap in the stated smooth orientable theorem. This is an internal research judgment, open to correction; no independent expert review, absolute priority certificate, journal acceptance or citation forecast is claimed.

The contribution is a topological obstruction: exact commutation of the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map and boundary Laplacian forces genus zero. In particular, it excludes every positive genus with one or two boundary components, answering both cases of an explicit published open problem. The two boundary cases, the capping construction and the resulting classification are consolidated in one manuscript.

## Existing tools and precise attribution

The finite-dimensional obstruction to harmonic conjugation is established inverse-problem theory. [Belishev and Korikov, arXiv:2112.14816v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.14816v2), Section 2, formulas (12)-(16), gives the exact genus rank for a related Hilbert transform and attributes it to Belishev's 2003 SIAM paper. Those pages were read directly. The original 2003 full proof was not read; its bibliographic record and the explicit primary attribution were checked. The manuscript gives a self-contained proof of its weaker rank bound and of its holomorphic trace criterion. Neither the period defect nor the trace algebra is presented as new.

[Tener, arXiv:1608.02095v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02095v1), Theorem 2.33, supplies smooth conformal sewing on general bordered Riemann surfaces. This is the author preprint of the 2017 Communications in Mathematical Physics article, and the citation distinguishes preprint theorem numbering. [Henriques and Tener, arXiv:2410.05929v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05929v1), Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 3.6, give the disk and local annulus welding used for the cap. The complete proof of Theorem 3.1 and the smooth nonzero-derivative statement in Theorem 3.2 were read. The manuscript explains the local collar reduction and proves the energy and boundary-metric consequences. It does not depend on a spin or conformal-field-theory assertion.

The boundary trace theorem, the Poincare inequality with nonempty Dirichlet boundary portion, the variational existence theorem, and smooth harmonic regularity are standard analytic inputs. The argument principle needed for the degree calculation is derived explicitly from Stokes' theorem and local zero multiplicities.

## Substantive step and significance

A finite-rank Hilbert-transform defect does not by itself force genus zero. Exact commutation first makes that defect scalar on each real Fourier plane, so only finitely many planes can fail the determinant identity. The algebra of holomorphic traces then links different frequencies: multiplying adjacent high modes produces a nonzero mode-one trace, except in a case resolved by a removable-singularity quotient. This supplies a globally one-to-one holomorphic map even when the initial surface was allowed arbitrary genus.

The remaining boundary count is handled by a geometric construction with an exact variational formula. Arclength welding to a Euclidean disk preserves the residual commutator under a Schur complement. Coercivity comes from the uncapped boundary, so the proof covers the constant mode and does not assume that the disk operator is invertible. Genus is preserved during the reduction.

These steps resolve the full remaining topological question in the published classification, over all smooth metrics in the orientable class. The method connects boundary operator symmetry to multiplicative holomorphic traces and to conformal sewing. That combination gives a reasoned case for serious spectral-geometry, analysis and complex-analysis review at the program's intended standard. The theorem is concise, depends on substantial classical tools, and concerns a specific exact-commutation problem; a journal may assess its scope more narrowly. Its suitability for Advances in Mathematics or Transactions of the AMS cannot be established by an internal audit.
