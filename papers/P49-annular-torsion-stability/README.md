# Sharp stability of the annulus for physical torsion

**Henry Zweiman. September 15, 2026.**

This preprint gives sharp quadratic stability for planar physical torsion with finitely many Lipschitz holes. The deficit controls the outer domain and hole union by two disks with a common center. It also gives a universal individual-asymmetry constant when the hole area is at most two-thirds of the outer area. The proof combines an exact plateau deficit with fixed-center level-set transport and the global calibration theorem of Hensel and Laux, crediting the transport viewpoint of Guerra, Machado and Ramos.
