## Contribution and internal decision

Provisionally admit this as one substantial research preprint under the revised program standard, conditional on completing the public artifact transaction. The proposed theorem gives global sharp quadratic stability for planar physical torsion with any finite number of Lipschitz holes. It controls the outer domain and hole union by disks with one common center, with a constant depending only on their area ratio. Separate individual asymmetries have a universal constant when the hole area is at most two-thirds of the outer area. A smooth second-harmonic perturbation proves exponent optimality. These conclusions and the transport proposition form one paper.

The problem is explicit and current: [Amato-Barbato's final article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00526-026-03362-w), Section 7, page 30, asks for the optimal exponents in its two stability theorems. The principal proposed advances are quantitative accounting for the positive-measure plateaus and fixed-center propagation through the whole prescribed range of areas. The argument applies without diameter, component-count or boundary-regularity constants. Its intermediate mass-window estimate is a possible reusable tool for elliptic problems with constrained flat regions.

This is a significance judgment, not an acceptance or citation prediction. The result is planar and linear, and uses strong recent external geometric input. Editors could regard the adaptation of recent transport methods as narrower than the intended Advances in Mathematics/Transactions of the AMS standard. Internal admission is revisable. No independent expert has reviewed the argument.
