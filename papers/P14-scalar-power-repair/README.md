# Scalar recurrence sequences with finitely repairable power subsequences

**Henry Zweiman — September 8, 2026**

[Complete Markdown manuscript](manuscript.md) · [PDF](manuscript.pdf) · [LaTeX](manuscript.tex)

A finite algebraic classification for scalar power subsequences of integral recurrences. It includes repeated roots, periodic cancellations, and finite transients; identifies the exact coefficient-field exponent in the nondegenerate case; proves eventual periodicity of the admissible exponents; and gives a terminating least-multiplier computation for sampling powers greater than one.

**Status:** complete manuscript with internal proof, source, and artifact checks. It is a proposed research contribution, not externally peer reviewed. Historical priority and journal-level significance remain provisional. The problem is formulated as a classification question here, not attributed to an invented named conjecture.
